We promote the knowledge, study and dissemination of mineralogy and the earth sciences, and foster their scientific outreach.

About us

FMF - Fundació Mineralògica Fabre is a private non-profit foundation, established in Barcelona and registered with the Foundations Registry of the Generalitat de Catalunya.

Its purpose, set out in its bylaws, is to promote the knowledge, study and dissemination of mineralogy and the earth sciences in general, highlighting their relevance to society and fostering their study and scientific outreach.
