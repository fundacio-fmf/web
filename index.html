FMFFundació Mineralògica Fabre

We promote the knowledge, study and dissemination of mineralogy and the earth sciences, and foster their scientific outreach.

Mission & vision

Our mission is to promote the knowledge, study and dissemination of mineralogy and the earth sciences, highlighting their relevance to society and fostering their scientific outreach and education.

Our vision is to become a reference point for the appreciation of mineralogy and the earth sciences, making mineralogical knowledge and heritage accessible to a broad audience.

What we do

The foundation supports long-running outreach projects that bring mineralogy to a wide community: two reference online forums and a virtual museum of mineral specimens.

The foundation
